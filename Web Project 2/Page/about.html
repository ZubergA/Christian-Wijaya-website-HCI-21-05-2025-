Our Story

The philosophy behind Christian Wijaya

Redefining Luxury

Founded in 2010, Christian Wijaya has established itself as a premier luxury fashion house known for its exquisite craftsmanship and sustainable practices. Our journey began with a simple vision: to create timeless pieces that transcend trends while respecting our planet.

Today, our designs are worn by discerning clients worldwide who appreciate the perfect balance between heritage and innovation.

Meet The Designer

Christian Wijaya

Founder & Creative Director

With over two decades in the fashion industry, Christian brings a unique perspective that blends his Indonesian heritage with global influences. His designs have been celebrated for their architectural precision and emotional resonance.

"Fashion should make you feel as beautiful as it makes you look. That's the essence of true luxury."
